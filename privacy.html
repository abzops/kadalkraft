Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This notice explains how information is used when you contact Kadalkraft through this website.

Your enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a message in your email app. It does not upload the form fields to a website database. You choose whether to send the email. We use the information you send, such as your name, company, email address and product requirements, to respond to your enquiry and discuss business with you.

Email and WhatsApp

Email and WhatsApp links open the relevant external service. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy notices. Avoid sending sensitive personal information through an initial product enquiry.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising trackers, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Contact us

For questions about information you have shared with us, contact info@kadalkraft.com.
