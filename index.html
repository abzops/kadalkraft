Before you enquire

What information should I share?

Include the seafood species, size or count, product format, packing, approximate quantity and destination. If you have a specification sheet, attach it in your email.

Can I discuss a specific product or shipment?

Yes. Share your requirements with us by email or WhatsApp. Availability, specifications, commercial terms and shipment details need to be discussed and confirmed for your enquiry.

Where is Kadalkraft based?

We are based in Kochi, India. Contact us at info@kadalkraft.com or 9400568744.
